# User guide

## Getting started

- **Students** create an account at **/register** with their name, email, matriculation number and password, then confirm their email.
- **Supervisors, technicians and administrators** receive accounts from the administrator. If no password was set, they get an email to choose one.
- **Forgot password:** click _Forgot password?_ on the sign-in page. A reset link will be emailed to you.
- **Profile:** open _My Profile_ from the sidebar or the account menu to update your details, photo and password. Technicians can mark themselves unavailable here.
- **Notifications:** the bell icon shows unread notifications. Click one to open the related report. _Notifications_ in the sidebar shows your full history, where you can filter unread, mark items read or unread, and delete them.

## Student

1. **Dashboard:** shows your total, pending, in-progress and resolved reports and your most recent complaints.
2. **Report an Issue:**
   - Choose a category and a building.
   - Optionally pick a listed room or facility.
   - Enter the specific location, a short title and a description.
   - Attach up to 5 photographs.
   - Submit. A reference number such as `CMR-2026-01001` is shown; keep it for follow-up.
3. **My Reports:** search by title, reference or location, and filter by status or category.
4. **Tracking a report:**
   - A progress bar shows the current stage.
   - The timeline lists every status change, with supervisor and technician updates.
   - Resolution details show the technician's completion notes, completion photographs and the date the complaint was resolved.
   - You can cancel a report while it is still awaiting review or assignment.

## Supervisor

1. **Dashboard:** shows totals for pending, assigned, in-progress, awaiting-verification and resolved complaints, new complaints, jobs to verify, and technician workload.
2. **All Complaints:** filter by status, category, building or priority, and search. Open a complaint to:
   - **Approve** it, optionally adjusting the category and priority, or **Reject** it with a reason. The student is notified either way.
   - **Assign a technician.** Technicians whose specialization matches the category are listed first, along with how many active jobs each has. You can add instructions.
   - **Reassign** a job to a different technician at any point before verification.
   - **Categorise or prioritise** the complaint, **Update status**, or post an update. Updates can be marked _internal_ to hide them from the student.
   - Review the reported photographs and the assignment history.
3. **Verification:** each job shows the completion notes and before/after photographs.
   - **Approve & resolve** marks the complaint resolved.
   - **Return for correction** sends the job back to the technician with your feedback.
4. **Technicians:** lists each technician's specializations, department, availability and active jobs.
5. **Assignment History:** lists every assignment and reassignment.

## Technician

1. **Dashboard:** shows your assigned, pending, in-progress and completed jobs, and alerts you to returned jobs.
2. **My Jobs:** tabs for pending, in-progress, returned and awaiting-verification jobs, sorted by priority.
3. **Job details:** shows the location, description, reported photographs and supervisor instructions. To work through a job:
   - **Accept job.** This step is optional.
   - **Start maintenance work.** The student is notified.
   - **Record work progress** as you go.
   - **Complete job:** add completion notes and photographs, then **Submit for verification**.
   - If the supervisor returns the job, read the feedback, choose **Start rework** and submit again.
4. **Job History:** lists jobs you have completed and submitted.

## Administrator

1. **Dashboard:** shows user counts by role, report totals, resolution rate and average resolution time, plus a status chart, recent report activity and an administrative audit log.
2. **Users:**
   - Search and filter by role or status.
   - **Register user:** any role. Specializations can be set for technicians.
   - **Edit:** change details, role, department or specializations.
   - **Activate or deactivate** accounts.
   - **Reset access:** send a reset email or set a temporary password.
3. **Departments:** create, edit, deactivate or delete departments; **Assign staff** to them in bulk; manage technician specializations.
4. **Configuration:** manage maintenance categories (each linked to a responsible department and a required specialization), campus buildings, and locations or facilities. Deactivated items are hidden from forms, but existing reports keep them.
5. **Reports & Analytics:**
   - Filter by date range and department.
   - View status, category, department, building and priority breakdowns, the monthly trend and technician performance.
   - Export the report list or a system summary as CSV, or print/save as PDF.
